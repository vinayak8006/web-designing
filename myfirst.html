ABES Engineering College

Ghaziabad Delhi , NCR

ABES Engineering College (ABES EC) is a private institution located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. Established in 2000, it offers a wide range of programs, including B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, and MBA, with a focus on academic excellence and industry readiness. The college has received NAAC A Grade accreditation and holds NBA accreditation for select programs. It is recognized for its commitment to innovation and has a vibrant campus life with numerous activities and co-curricular programs. The college also provides scholarships and placement support, with a notable placement record and a highest offer of INR 59.91 LPA in the 2024-25 placement season.
